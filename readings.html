Reading Response #001: In Plato's Cave

Photography alone is considered to be the most realistic portrayal of reality that can be reproduced with modern technology. Recently I have realized my obsession with recreating reality through my photography. I then began to ask myself; what version of reality is my reality? I never wanted to edit my photos with filters that altered the reality I wanted to portray; unless my reality was distorted by my awful phone camera. It is very frustrating when the most convenient medium in your pocket doesn’t see what you want it to see. At the same time I laugh at myself for thinking like this because this is the essence of interpretation. You can meticulously critique your work repeatedly until you get; what in your eyes, is the perfect version. Yet not everyone will see it the way you saw it. This is not to say that you shouldn’t create or recreate the beauty you see. Simply remember that there is more than one way to bring your reality to life. If it is interpreted differently then you may have inspired them to create their own version of your reality.

I think this aggression or fixation to recreate reality is because in most cases photography has been seen to be a means to an end. Meaning it has historically been used for things like news, evidence or to capture moments in time. Susan Sontag speaks more about this in an excerpt from “In Plato’s Cave”. I also think that the name of the text can also be interpreted as the relationship between humans and their technological advancements. What I mean is as technology progresses, there are people that utilize its advancements and those who avoid it. When it comes to image making with artificial intelligence, my natural reaction is to avoid it. I had the similar thought process when it came to image editing but upon further review I realized I was once again thinking in my old ways. I now realize that aslong as the creator of any art piece has the intention for their piece to be perceived as art it is considered art.

I found that editing the images for assignment 2 was very fun as they allowed me to accentuate the elements of the photos that drew me to it in the first place. While editing, I found that I imposed the asthtetics I had seen in the past that I admired. I turned the exit sign photo blue because it reminded me of theatrical lighting or some lights that I would see at a concert. I think it is beautiful the way that the lights in this photo work or don’t work together. I took the second photo because I thought it was the most unique thing out of the firehose. I really liked the image beforehand because of how much detail there was on it, you were able to see all the dust that had accumulated as well as the thick coating of paint chipping off. The reading and assignment worked together because it allowed me to practice and learn about photography in a new light.

Reading Response #002: AI Slop

The article “With ‘AI slop’ distorting our reality, the world is sleepwalking into disaster” by Nesrine Malik accurately describes whe hold that AI has on a lot of people. I especially related to the line where Malik mentioned the effect it has on one of their older relatives. I agree that I may be a bit addicted to social media, I try my best to avoid viewing political content or news from there. I also have an older relative who didn’t grow up using technology. He sees the crazy AI videos of Donald Trump jumping on a trampoline with a goat and is delightendly entertained. When my older relatives show me things like this I know they’re not real but I can’t help but wonder if they do too. It is quite miraculous to see how advanced AI is when it comes to making things look realistic. I should have known better than to think a bunch of bunnies hopping on a trampoline was too good to be true. I also agree with Malik that if we see something that we want to be real we are more likely to believe it, which in turn forms a distrust in everything we see over the internet. Therefore, naturally I have formed a growing distrust and antagonism against AI content and AI art. As someone who is pursuing a career in the arts; it's frightening to see companies choose to make an AI advertisement instead of hiring a real person to do it. I want to overcome my natural distrust for artificial Intelegence but the more I want to get away from it the more the topic and content surround me, in class rooms, on the subway, and on my social media feed. Overcoming it sooner rather than later would allow me to more easily adapt to the constantly growing world around me. I don’t want to be the person who gets left behind because she was too scared of the new technology. I don’t want to be the person who blames AI for stealing our jobs. It is difficult to have a growth mindset when you strongly disagree with the technology that is supposed to help you grow. Especially when it's being used to deceive people in such a deveus way, and is associated with so much content you so strongly disagree with. As I grow with AI I will use it to make my life simpler, not so much so that I rely on it to do every little thing. I will use it to help me with my creative vision; using its knowledge to help me show the world through my eyes and bringing it to life at my hands. I will use it to become a better candidate for the job I dream of. Through hard work and only the assistance of AI.
